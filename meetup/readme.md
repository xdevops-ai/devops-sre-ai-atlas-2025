## Latest meetup — purpose & focus

- **Theme:** The End of YAML? Managing Infrastructure with Natural Language & Knowledge Graphs
- **Goal:**  
  Introduce a new paradigm where infrastructure is managed as *knowledge* rather than static files — using ontologies, knowledge graphs, and AI as a safe interface.
- **Key topics:**
  - Why YAML and file-based IaC are breaking down at scale
  - Ontology-driven infrastructure modeling (RDF/OWL, knowledge graphs)
  - Natural language → validated operations (deterministic, policy-aware)
  - Self-healing infrastructure and drift reconciliation
  - Fixing the support escalation spiral
- **Live demo:**  
  Querying, inspecting, and reasoning about infrastructure using natural language and a knowledge graph (YAML vs Knowledge Graph comparison).
- **Discussion focus:**  
  Trust, safety, maturity, and real-world limits of AI-driven infrastructure.

## Emerging theme — AI Shadow Operations

A recurring discussion point during the meetup was **AI Shadow usage**:
- Engineers already use AI daily for debugging, fixes, and decisions
- Most of this knowledge remains personal, ephemeral, and untracked
- Organizations lose long-term value and learning

Upcoming sessions will explore:
- How to make AI usage visible, shared, and explainable
- Moving from hero-driven fixes to systems that remember
- Turning individual AI assistance into institutional knowledge
