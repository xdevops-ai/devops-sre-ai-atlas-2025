## November 2026 directory refresh

The [Platform Directory]({{ "/platforms/" | relative_url }}) now includes nine additional operational platforms, reviews every previous record, and distinguishes GA, preview, lifecycle transitions and unknowns. The [current capability matrix]({{ "/capabilities/" | relative_url }}) uses source-backed descriptions rather than inherited maturity rankings.

## Read the market by operational need

- **Investigate incidents:** compare packaged AI SRE tools with observability-native assistants.
- **Execute operational work:** distinguish governed workflows, recommendations, pull requests and production writes.
- **Build agents:** keep frameworks and data-agent platforms separate from turnkey SRE products.
- **Assess risk:** check feature availability, permissions, integrations and evidence before treating a vendor claim as a deployed capability.

## What changed in this review

- Added Robusta/HolmesGPT, NeuBird, Resolve AI, Traversal, Ciroos, Cleric, AWS DevOps Agent, Komodor/Klaudia and Grafana Assistant.
- Re-indexed existing records including Rootly, incident.io, Kubiya and Azure SRE Agent, rather than creating duplicates.
- Corrected lifecycle and identity transitions; retained legacy URLs and explicitly marked unresolved claims.
- Kept the existing six-axis framework, with descriptive evidence instead of newly invented numerical rankings.
