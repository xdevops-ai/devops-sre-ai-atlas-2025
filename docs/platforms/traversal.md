## Scope and limitations

Traversal describes causal investigation, alert intelligence and a production context model with a Knowledge Bank. Its homepage describes diagnosing and fixing problems, while its security page says read-only always. This conflict remains unresolved: investigation and remediation recommendations are described here, but autonomous production writes are not verified. Vendor benchmarks are not independent customer outcomes.
