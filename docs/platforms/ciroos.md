# Ciroos

**Evidence reviewed: 14 November 2026.** Official documentation/announcements; no independent runtime test.

## Identity and availability

documented commercial AI SRE with Teams setup guide; Signal Intelligence product announcement undated in fetched page.

## Is it relevant to your team?

**Why shortlist it:** Evaluate cross-domain alert correlation and investigations within collaborative response channels.

**Pilot question:** Can channel/project/cluster scope and account linking prevent the wrong team from receiving another project's findings?

This is editorial evaluation guidance inferred from the documented scope, not a product guarantee. Confirm contract, region, deployment model and feature entitlement with the provider.

## Scope and limitations

Ciroos describes cross-domain investigation, alert deduplication and correlation using persistent graph context. Its Teams guide requires an active account and defines channel, project and cluster scope, account linking, war rooms and human-reviewed recommendations. Signal Intelligence describes an actuator for remediation, but that architecture statement does not establish general availability or the permissions and scope of every action.
