# Rootly — Incident Response Automation

**Evidence reviewed: 14 November 2026.** Official documentation/announcements; no independent runtime test.

## Identity and availability

Rootly AI SRE + incident platform; documented, MCP GA

## Is it relevant to your team?

**Why shortlist it:** Assess AI investigations alongside incident coordination, communication and incident history.

**Pilot question:** Can the response team inspect evidence and continue its normal incident workflow, including the required chat and MCP integrations?

This is editorial evaluation guidance inferred from the documented scope, not a product guarantee. Confirm contract, region, deployment model and feature entitlement with the provider.

## Scope and limitations

Rootly combines incident workflows with AI investigation and connected evidence. Teams and MCP surfaces are documented separately. Confirm the integrations and permissions required for your response workflow; incident automation does not establish general infrastructure provisioning.
