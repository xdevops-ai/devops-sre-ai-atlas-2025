## Scope and limitations

Cleric combines alert investigation, change verification and evidence-linked fix pull requests. Its product documentation describes read-only defaults, opt-in writes, delayed-regression detection, human review of fixes and recovery verification. Integrations and MCP provide additional context. Product demonstrations and pricing examples are not independent evidence of customer outcomes or unconditional autonomous remediation.
