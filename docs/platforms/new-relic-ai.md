# New Relic Autopilot

**Evidence reviewed: 14 November 2026.** Official documentation/announcements; no independent runtime test.

## Identity and availability

New Relic Autopilot, formerly SRE Agent, GA announcement 2026-07-21

## Is it relevant to your team?

**Why shortlist it:** Evaluate incident investigation and recommendations within an existing New Relic estate.

**Pilot question:** Does Autopilot explain a real incident with linked telemetry and runbook context, without presenting a recommendation as an executed fix?

This is editorial evaluation guidance inferred from the documented scope, not a product guarantee. Confirm contract, region, deployment model and feature entitlement with the provider.
